# L5-03 — Evidence-linked briefing opportunity

[All research examples](../../README.md#learn-by-researching)

## Problem

Combine market, technical and editorial evidence while leaving business, engineering and publication commitments with their owners.

## Exact prompt

Run this prompt in a consumer project with the skill selection it names.

```text
Use installed deep-research and research-evaluate with no Extension Pack. Investigate an evidence-linked technical briefing service as a strategic opportunity for a small team. Research three connected workstreams: the actual user/editorial problem and alternatives; technical feasibility of source-to-claim traceability and bounded refresh; and permitted source use, review and publication constraints. Begin with original product documentation, public editorial/research guidance, provenance standards and relevant papers. Recover original support behind trade-press claims. Engine repositories can inform technical feasibility but do not prove customer demand.

Separate observed evidence, supplier statements, value assumptions, untested demand and recommendations. Date and attribute any current prices or terms; obtain no new paid access and claim no legal clearance. Produce coordinated handoffs to Business Building, Software Engineering and creative/editorial production, including disconfirming evidence and the smallest next experiments. Do not launch a business, contact people or publish externally. Demonstrate a scoped refresh of one changing input while retaining unrelated valid work.

Allow 30 searches and 96 source reads/actions total, with finalisation and audit reserved and no new paid agents or purchases. Write research/l5-03/research.md, programme-map.md, opportunity-assumptions.md, handoff-business.md, handoff-software.md, handoff-editorial.md, change-record.md and evaluation/audit.md beneath research/l5-03/. Record exact source/support links, permissions, observation dates, uncertainty and revision-specific audits. Preserve prior accepted records. Return actual paths and conditional findings, not a fabricated validated market or authority to make downstream commitments.
```

## Evaluation contract

Evaluate programme consistency, competing constraints, disconfirming evidence and preserved downstream judgement.

Conditional opportunity, permitted use and scoped refresh; FX09/FX11/TM03. Core, business/software/editorial handoffs.

Review the actual source access, evidence and claim support, temporal scope, uncertainty, resource bounds and preservation required by the prompt. Distinguish producer self-checks from fixed-input independent evaluation. A passing structural check does not establish research quality.
