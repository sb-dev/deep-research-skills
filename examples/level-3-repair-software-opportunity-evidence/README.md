# L3-02 — Repair-software opportunity evidence

[All research examples](../../README.md#learn-by-researching)

## Problem

Separate observed repair activity from untested market demand and willingness to pay.

## Exact prompt

Run this prompt in a consumer project with the skill selection it names.

```text
Use installed deep-research and research-evaluate with no Extension Pack. Produce a decision-ready evidence report on whether a small UK team should investigate software for community repair organisations. Begin with original Open Repair Alliance documentation/data and publications by participating organisations. Use UK official statistics only where the population and definitions match the question. Research the operational problem, existing alternatives, plausible users and technical/data constraints. Separate observed activity from unmet software demand, procurement authority, willingness to pay and representativeness. Organisational or supplier statements are claims to appraise, not independent demand validation. Do not invent interviews, forecasts or a market-size estimate.

Recommend the smallest next evidence-gathering steps and explicit conditions that would reverse the recommendation. Starting a business, contacting people and spending money remain consumer decisions. Use no more than 12 searches and 36 source reads/actions, aggregated across work, with review reserved. Use existing authorised tools only; no additional paid access or external contact.

Write research/l3-02/research.md, research/l3-02/opportunity-assumptions.md and research/l3-02/evaluation/audit.md. Preserve question-to-evidence/claim support, original locators, observed periods, contrary evidence and unresolved demand assumptions. Audit the exact submission without silently repairing it. Read existing work before revision and preserve unaffected evidence. Return actual paths and a conditional recommendation, not a fabricated validated business opportunity or installed benchmark result.
```

## Expected artefacts

- `research/l3-02/research.md`
- `research/l3-02/opportunity-assumptions.md`
- `research/l3-02/evaluation/audit.md`

## Evaluation contract

Evaluate decision relevance without unsupported demand claims.

Activity does not prove demand or willingness to pay; FX09/FX11. Core only.

Review the actual source access, evidence and claim support, temporal scope, uncertainty, resource bounds and preservation required by the prompt. Distinguish producer self-checks from fixed-input independent evaluation. A passing structural check does not establish research quality.

Benchmark mapping: `EX-L3-02`; L03/L05/L09; K01/K05/K15. See the [evaluation layers and quality dimensions](../../docs/04-testing-and-benchmark-spec.md).
