### Alternatives considered

| Policy | Useful aspect | Failure if used alone | Decision |

## 1. Research effort tiers

An effort tier chooses the scope of work and the cheapest adequate representation. It is not a quality grade, confidence score, skill-installation mode or progressive-example level. All tiers preserve truthful source access, uncertainty and claim support. A task may start directly at the tier its method requires; the earlier representations can be reused without performing ceremonial passes through every tier.

| Tier | Appropriate responsibility | Cheapest useful work and required record | Completion / escalation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Orientation | Clarify an unfamiliar question, discover source classes, assess feasibility or produce an explicitly requested source map. | Brief, limited metadata/quick-source scan and question-to-source map. Record leads as leads and identify the required method, permissions and next gaps. | Complete only the authorised orientation output. A request for a supported answer is not satisfied by a list of promising sources; proceed to T2/T3 or resolve a prerequisite. |
| T2 Bounded verification | Answer a narrow factual question, check a named source or repair one supported proposition. | Native/exact retrieval, relevant passage/data/region, compact evidence-to-claim record and targeted support/freshness check. Use broader discovery only when the original cannot be resolved adequately. | Stop after the bounded question and necessary contrary/context checks are addressed. A comparison, causal interpretation or multiple material unresolved questions requires broader analysis, not merely more prose. |
| T3 Focused synthesis | Compare a defined set of alternatives or produce a decision-ready report with several questions and complementary evidence. | Source/coverage map, focused acquisition batches, evidence table, independence/contradiction analysis, targeted follow-up, synthesis and exact-report review. | Complete when the method-specific coverage and material support gates pass. Escalate the method or reviewer for consequential inferences that generic synthesis cannot justify. |
| T4 Specialist or comprehensive investigation | A formal exhaustive/search-protocol claim, specialist appraisal, complex original analysis or high-consequence judgement requiring expertise. | Explicit protocol and specialist source/review obligations, piloted extraction where required, documented coverage, appropriate analysis and qualified assurance. The existing compact artifacts may expand as responsibility warrants. | The named method must actually be performed. Missing expertise, access, authority or necessary capacity blocks that obligation. A large source count or a provider report is not a substitute. |

T4 is not always more searching. A small but consequential calculation can require specialist review with little additional retrieval. Conversely, a broad descriptive map can be large without supporting a causal claim. Breadth, inspection fidelity, analytical complexity and assurance are selected separately within the tier. A known exact fact may need a high-fidelity figure inspection while remaining a T2 research scope.

### Disposition of all eight candidate effort steps

| ID | Candidate step | Role in the adopted strategy |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | question framing | Required before material acquisition in every tier; reuse the approved brief rather than continually reopening it. |
| E02 | metadata / quick-source scan | Cheap T1 discovery or triage within another tier; cannot establish unread source content. |
| E03 | source map | Makes expected source classes, independence and access gaps explicit; proportionate to the question. |
| E04 | focused retrieval | Acquires the exact material needed for support after selection; common to T2–T4. |
| E05 | evidence table | A compact section, table or structured record that exposes support/contrary evidence before polished synthesis. |
| E06 | targeted verification | Checks material claims, context, freshness and uncertain extraction; applied before report polish or a broad rerun. |
| E07 | full synthesis | Justified when integration answers the brief; avoid generating a long report for a bounded source fact. |
| E08 | specialist / exhaustive research only when justified | Triggered by the method, risk or unresolved inferential need, not prestige, source count or a desire to use more agents. |

These dispositions retain every candidate without making the sequence rigid. For example, a source map discovered while retrieving can improve the plan, but a newly attractive source does not silently change the agreed research question.

## 2. Escalation rules

Before a more expensive operation, record the unresolved question/claim, what result could change the answer, why the cheaper adequate alternatives are insufficient, expected access/fidelity, bounded action, likely resource commitment and required authority. This can be a short entry in the Stage 5 plan or gap record. No generic workflow platform is needed.

| ID | Trigger | Permitted escalation and retained boundary |
|---|---|---|
| X01 | Unknown or ambiguous question | Clarify scope and intended use before expensive retrieval. A consequential ambiguity is for the requester, not a model assumption. |
| X02 | Metadata is relevant but cannot support the claim | Retrieve the actual original passage, full methods or required subset. If unavailable, record an access gap; do not upgrade the metadata. |
| X03 | Repeated reporting or insufficient source coverage | Seek a genuinely distinct origin or missing relevant source class; do not pay for another wrapper around the same material. |
| X04 | Garbled text, table layout or decisive media context | Inspect the original region first; use OCR, transcription or a specialist parser only when needed and validate the derived result. |
| X05 | Material contradiction or unsupported inference | Target the discriminating definition, period, measurement or premise. Escalate to a qualified method/reviewer when that is the missing capability. |
| X06 | Changing evidence or an unmet freshness requirement | Recheck the affected source/version or dated record; retain still-valid historical support. A full rerun needs a broader reason. |
| X07 | A formal method or consequential use exceeds current assurance | Add the required specialist review or revise the method with the appropriate owner. A second model pass is not independent human expertise. |
| X08 | Current allowance cannot cover the required next action and finalisation | Propose the specific extra authority/capacity or an explicitly limited output. Do not spend beyond the ceiling or mark an unmet prerequisite complete. |
| X09 | Parallel execution might resolve independent gaps | Partition independent work, share the parent cap, assign non-overlapping bounded tasks and account for synthesis/review. Parallelism is optional, not a default skill architecture. |

Escalation is justified by a plausible material effect on support, coverage, uncertainty or necessary review. It need not promise a changed conclusion: verifying that a decisive contrary finding does not apply can be useful. Optional stylistic polish, another near-duplicate source and a higher-priced provider without a diagnosed capability gap do not justify additional research by themselves.

Within existing scope, authorised tools and remaining allowance, the researcher may change the path or tier without asking for ceremonial approval. New expense, processor disclosure, specialist engagement, changed outcome requirements or reopened approved decisions require their real owner. A tier label never grants those permissions. A cheaper method may be selected only when it meets the same required inference and reporting standard, or when the owner explicitly changes that requirement.

### Scheduling the next useful operation

Prioritise mandatory missing support and high-consequence uncertainty before optional breadth or polish. Among adequate alternatives, prefer the smaller, more direct, reusable and checkable operation. Record why the chosen operation is expected to reduce the specific uncertainty; do not invent a probability-of-success or monetary value-of-information score.

Use a bounded batch when several independent leads can be triaged together. At its completion, record relevant new origins, resolved or newly discovered material gaps, important counterevidence and resources consumed or still committed. This is a decision checkpoint, not an obligation to run another batch. Do not keep a repeated failed route alive by renaming the query or launching another agent.

## 3. Stopping rules

**Stopping acquisition, having sufficient evidence, passing review and delivering a report are different events.** The Stage 5 review and delivery gates still apply after a stop decision. Reserve enough authorised capacity to audit, document limitations and close the current work safely; the fact that collection stopped cannot waive those actions.

### Assess every specified stopping criterion

| ID | Candidate criterion | Decision semantics and evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | all required subquestions have evidence | Inspect the brief-to-claim map. Each required question must have adequate support or an explicitly authorised limitations outcome. A neighbouring answer or unsearched field cannot pass as coverage. |
| S02 | material claims have adequate direct support | Check the actual proposition, source/representation, locator, inferential premises and relevant independence. Presence of citations is not entailment. Unsupported assertions must be repaired, narrowed or withheld. |
| S03 | major source classes are represented | Evaluate the classes required by this question and method, not all thirteen classes in every task. Missing relevant classes need legitimate completion or a disclosed method/coverage limitation. |
| S04 | new searches yield diminishing relevant evidence | Compare distinct, justified attempts and new relevant origins/gaps. Repeating one corpus or retrieving duplicates is not evidence of global saturation. This signal never overrides a known material source or mandatory search obligation. |
| S05 | important contradictions are explained or explicitly unresolved | Preserve the dispute and its consequences. An authorised contested finding can pass with suitable uncertainty; an unresolved conflict blocking the required conclusion cannot be renamed a minor caveat. |
| S06 | freshness requirement is satisfied | Verify the claim's required period/version and actual recheck basis. Retrieval date alone is insufficient; unknown currentness remains an explicit limitation. |
| S07 | budget / time ceiling reached with limitations reported | Stop further unreserved collection at the cap. Record consumed/committed resources and unanswered needs. This is a resource-stop reason, not evidence sufficiency or permission to deliver an inadequate substitute. |

### Decision outcomes and precedence

Use the following decision order at each material checkpoint; the exact labels are local policy vocabulary, not a universal runtime API.

1. **BLOCKED:** a mandatory owner decision, required permission, unresolved consequential action result or essential prerequisite prevents legitimate continuation. Preserve evidence and ask the precise question; do not continue another bootstrap stage.
2. **STOP_SUFFICIENT:** the evidence gate for the authorised output passes and the required finalisation/review has passed. Do not consume leftover collection capacity merely because it exists. Delivery still needs the Stage 5 audience/receipt checks.
3. **CONTINUE_REVIEW:** evidence is adequate, but necessary finalisation remains and its scope/resources are authorised. Use the reserved capacity for the exact report and support audit, not more optional discovery. If the required review cannot be completed, BLOCKED applies instead.
4. **CONTINUE** or **ESCALATE:** evidence is not yet adequate and a specified, useful, bounded next action fits the remaining authority and capacity. The latter means a materially different tier/fidelity/method inside already-approved bounds. Low recent yield alone cannot block a known discriminating source.
5. **STOP_LIMITED:** no useful authorised action remains or a resource boundary has been reached, and the brief already permits an explicit limitations output. Record the exact deficiencies and the stop reason; necessary review and audience checks still apply. Otherwise the outcome is BLOCKED for the needed decision, not silent scope reduction.

The policy does not require perfect certainty. It requires that the delivered conclusion and its uncertainty match the support and intended use. A finding that an answer cannot presently be established may itself be valid research; it is not a substitute for a definitive answer the requester explicitly made mandatory. Similarly, a research-level limitations report cannot mark an unfinished bootstrap stage complete.

### Diminishing returns without fake recall guarantees

The plan names the source/search boundary, what constitutes a materially new item, and the permitted follow-up checkpoint. At each checkpoint, distinguish duplicate records, repeated underlying observations, new contextual detail, new contrary evidence and actual closure of a required gap. Record the number of inspected candidates when available, but do not equate an unmeasured recall percentage with completeness.

For bounded or purposive work, a justified stopping judgement can use repeated low-yield searches across the intended diverse routes once material gaps have been resolved or explicitly accepted. A single failed source or repeated query does not demonstrate this. For a method requiring exhaustive discovery within a declared corpus, execute that protocol and all mandated source/selection steps; low yield cannot waive them. A hard ceiling that prevents completion means the method is incomplete, not that it has become exhaustive by definition.

An optional additional route may be declined without asking a question when it is outside the needed evidence scope and all gates already pass. Conversely, a known potentially decisive source cannot be ignored merely to reach the next stage faster. The stopping decision records that distinction in the plan/gap/review artifacts.

## 4. Budget controls

### Nine cost categories

| ID | Category | Unit / commitment to track | Cheap-first control and caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| K01 | search calls | Calls/pages/queries within the selected service allowance; separately track useful coverage. | Scope and batch discovery, use known-source retrieval, and stop duplicate searches. A low call count does not establish high quality. |
| K02 | browser actions | Sessions, navigations/actions, rendering time and service charges where applicable. | Prefer direct/native access when adequate; isolate necessary interaction and never reuse or log secrets as evidence. |
| K03 | premium API usage | Provider-specific billable units, currency, quota and accepted in-flight liability. | Estimate with current authorised account information; bound the operation before submission. Unknown marginal charges are not assumed free. |
| K04 | full-document retrieval | Documents, bytes/pages, transfer/storage and any purchase or access fee. | Triage metadata first; inspect only the needed representation/range when the method allows. Do not mistake partial reading for full review. |
| K05 | OCR / multimodal analysis | Pages, images, frames/intervals or service jobs, plus required checking. | Inspect the original where possible; perform only the transformation needed to resolve the gap, preserving originals and fidelity limits. |
| K06 | code execution | Jobs, CPU/memory/storage or hosted-runtime units and necessary analysis review. | Reuse verified inputs; execute the smallest appropriate calculation before an expensive model or corpus job. Arithmetic precision is not causal validity. |
| K07 | provider deep-research runs | Runs, configured tool/token bounds, exposed subcalls, retained storage and outstanding jobs. | Use for a justified orchestration need, not every bounded fact; retain input/output IDs and reconcile actual cost when exposed. A finished report still needs audit. |
| K08 | parallel agents | Worker tasks, per-worker reservations, parent aggregate usage and reconciliation overhead. | Allocate disjoint bounded work; cap parent exposure before launch. More agents do not create independent evidence or bypass quotas. |
| K09 | human specialist review | Agreed scope, appointments/hours/fee, review deliverables and authority. | Prepare a focused evidence package and precise unresolved questions; do not book or imply completed specialist review without authorisation and execution. |

These units form a **resource vector**, not numbers to add into one meaningless score. A provider run can contain searches: record both operational counts where exposed, but charge an underlying billable event only once. A wrapper/worker reservation is not another independent invoice. If billing is bundled, identify the known parent charge and state that subcost allocation is unavailable. Use actual bills/usage records for measured spend; documentary prices or estimates remain labelled estimates.

### Establish a finite work envelope

Before material work, the plan identifies permitted operations, relevant count/time/cash limits, existing entitlements, the owner of additional commitments and a finalisation reserve. A simple bounded task can use a small explicit batch limit rather than a complicated budget table. No universal tier-to-price mapping is imposed.

An unspecified overall cash budget is not unlimited permission. Existing authorised tools may be used within their known task/session controls without a new purchase, while separate paid calls, data purchases or human engagements remain unapproved. Record cash use as unknown or included in the existing entitlement where established; do not invent a zero monetary cost. If an action could create an additional unbounded or unknown charge, obtain a reliable bound or the required owner decision before launching it.

The planner can set an internal operational cap within already-delegated authority; this does not require the user to approve every search. The cap and its unit must be explicit before an iterative batch starts. If the request does not supply enough authority to choose a consequential bound, ask before that commitment. A task-specific deadline is recorded with its timezone and source; do not invent one from expected model latency.

For each constrained resource dimension:

```text
available_for_new_collection
  = ceiling - consumed - outstanding_reservations - finalisation_reserve
```

An operation may start only when its defensible upper bound fits **every applicable dimension**, including the parent aggregate. An operation explicitly accounts for every tracked applicable dimension; zero or not applicable needs an established basis, not an omitted field. If an exact upper bound is not known, use a conservative enforceable provider/tool limit or a smaller bounded operation; do not silently replace unknown with zero. The amount is a reservation until the result/cost is reconciled, not a claim that it has already been spent.

Finalisation may use its specifically reserved capacity; collection may not borrow it automatically. If review is larger than estimated, retain the unmet review obligation and resolve capacity rather than weakening the required check. When a ceiling is reached, do not launch more collection; use only legitimately available finalisation/cleanup capacity. If none remains, preserve the coherent partial work and report the blocker rather than fabricating completion.

### Reservation, reconciliation and interruption

A reservation record identifies operation, target gap, bounded units, parent allowance, status and any provider job/request ID. Avoid a new persistent accounting service: a table in the plan/provenance record is sufficient for current research. Parallel work reserves against one shared parent before dispatch; workers cannot each receive the entire remaining budget. Account for merge, deduplication and review work before choosing parallel execution.

On observed completion, replace the reservation with actual known consumption and release only the established unused amount. On a timeout or lost result, retain the outstanding exposure until the provider/destination is checked, cancellation is confirmed or the reserved maximum is conservatively treated as consumed. Cancellation is not assumed to refund completed work. Do not retry a potentially still-running expensive job simply because its response was lost.

Historical actual consumption never decreases when a new batch or refreshed report begins. A new authorised budget revision can increase a ceiling explicitly, but must not erase earlier spend or reset an in-flight commitment. Rate limits and account quotas are additional constraints, not the same thing as the research's own budget. Use permitted cache/batch routes and respect backoff; more accounts or workers are not a legitimate way to evade a shared limit.

### Illustrative arithmetic, not measured spend

A synthetic allocation has a ceiling of 100 resource units, 35 consumed, 20 outstanding and 15 reserved for finalisation. New collection has 30 units available. A proposed action bounded at 31 cannot start; one bounded at 30 can. Two workers each bounded at 20 cannot both start under the same 30-unit availability. These numbers are test inputs, not prices, recommended quotas or evidence of actual API expenditure. The companion verification executes this arithmetic and boundary behaviour.

## 5. Refresh and incremental-update policy

A refresh is a new bounded assessment of whether the existing evidence remains fit for its stated use. A correction repairs an error; a source relocation repairs access; a changed brief may require a new investigation. Do not treat all four as a complete report rerun or as merely advancing a date field. [Accepted Stage 3 temporal/provenance model and Stage 5 repair contracts.]

### Trigger and scope decisions

| ID | Trigger | Minimum justified response |
|---|---|---|
| U01 | The consumer asks for a later valid-as-of state or the planned freshness boundary is reached. | Inspect the required changing source/version or relevant update channel; retain verified stable historical claims. The check date is not automatically the new validity date. |
| U02 | New eligible evidence or a newly accessible original may affect a material claim. | Triage relevance and origin, then acquire only the needed support; re-evaluate dependent claims, uncertainty and any affected source coverage. |
| U03 | A correction, retraction, failed extraction or source-integrity concern appears. | Investigate the defect and its dependency scope promptly within authority; distinguish an erroneous extraction from an invalid source or changed applicability. |
| U04 | The question, population, criteria, method or downstream use materially changes. | Reopen the brief/method decision. Reuse applicable evidence, but do not label a fundamentally different task as a routine refresh or silently apply obsolete eligibility. |
| U05 | An unchanged source moves or access/retention permissions change. | Repair the locator/access record and check permitted use; unchanged byte content need not be re-analysed solely because the URL moved. Lost evidence can reduce auditability. |
| U06 | No material new information is found in the defined update search. | Record the actual scope and date of the check and the unchanged findings. Do not claim a global absence of new evidence or silently strengthen confidence. |

Select the trigger's smallest evidence unit and follow the Stage 5 dependency rules through claims, synthesis, report and review. If dependencies are uncertain, expand the relevant review boundary explicitly. A shared denominator, eligibility change or flawed original method can require a broad rerun; preservation protects valid work, not an artificially narrow correction.

### Refresh sequence and acceptance

1. Read the last issued revision, brief, source/claim records, review results, gaps, validity basis and remaining permissions. Identify why a refresh might matter before reacquiring the corpus.
2. Classify the trigger using U01–U06. Reconfirm the current question and authority. Record a new finite action envelope with historical consumption and outstanding liabilities preserved.
3. Map potentially affected claims and the source classes required to check them. Reuse authorised cached evidence only where the exact representation, validity and access rights still fit. Check missing/corrected records and previously unresolved relevant gaps.
4. Acquire or verify the smallest necessary delta using the selected method. For a protocol-governed review, complete its required update search; selective delta retrieval cannot be falsely described as that protocol.
5. Integrate rather than append: update relevant evidence, support relations, contradictions, uncertainty and the claim-to-question map. Retain an identifiable prior basis and unmodified unaffected records.
6. Re-run the appropriate evidence, budget, report-review and handoff gates. Record changed and unchanged findings, unresolved limitations and the actual valid-as-of basis. Notify previous recipients only when authorised and actually performed.

No default polling daemon, scheduled task or fixed cross-domain refresh interval is created. The consuming project may supply an existing schedule; the research skill provides the bounded update operation and trigger metadata. A repeatedly changing high-priority topic can justify a separately resourced ongoing method, but the method, review and scheduling authority must actually exist. Cochrane's editorial intervals in N04 are not a universal cache expiry or permission to ignore an urgent correction.

## Decision examples and verification design

The [policy cases](2026-09-10-stage-06-policy-cases.json) are explicitly synthetic. They give the pure decision checks concrete inputs and expected outcomes. Each declared evidence-assessment value represents the result of a required substantive check. Combined readiness is not inferred from a file name or a source count; the deterministic fixture checker only evaluates the consequences of those explicit test premises.

The cases contrast sufficient evidence with spare budget, missing review, an unapproved limited outcome, exhausted resources, a useful unresolved route despite low yield, justified escalation, optional redundant work, unavailable specialist review, missing authority and closure with explicit limits. Separate accounting cases test exact-cap admission, reserve protection, aggregate worker exposure and unresolved charges. Refresh cases distinguish a narrowly affected chain from a changed use requiring reframing.

Semantic review must check the policy itself, not only whether the program agrees with hand-written expectations: a limited stop cannot be reported sufficient; support and review remain separate; low yield cannot override known missing evidence; all dimensions of a budget constrain launch; unresolved charges remain committed; and a refresh cannot replace unverified earlier evidence with a newer-looking timestamp. The verifier is a small executable policy rehearsal in the research log, not an installed generic research scheduler.
