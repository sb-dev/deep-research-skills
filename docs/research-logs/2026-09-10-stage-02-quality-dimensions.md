# Stage 2 Candidate Quality Dimensions

This output proposes assessable dimensions from the professional comparison. It is not a scoring model, acceptance threshold, implemented evaluator or guarantee of research quality. The [source register](2026-09-10-stage-02-sources.md) and [failure taxonomy](2026-09-10-stage-02-failure-taxonomy.md) make the rationale inspectable.

| ID | Candidate dimension | Question an evaluator should answer | Inspectable evidence and counterexample | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K01 | Question and use fit | Does the finding answer the agreed need at the right level of consequence? | Brief-to-finding mapping; a comprehensive but irrelevant report fails. | S16; Stage 1 |
| K02 | Coverage honesty | Is the search/selection posture appropriate and described accurately? | Search boundaries and excluded material; a selective scan claiming completeness fails. | S02, S06 |
| K03 | Source access and provenance | Can the reader identify what was actually inspected and where it originated? | Locators, access level and meaningful transformations; an unseen source cited as verified fails. | S09, S35, S36 |
| K04 | Evidence-unit integrity | Are records, underlying studies/events and versions distinguished? | Linkage and counting rationale; multiple reports inflating support fails. | S03, S07 |
| K05 | Applicability and methodological fitness | Does the evidence support this kind of inference in this setting? | Appraisal appropriate to the claim; descriptive mapping offered as effect proof fails. | S08, S13 |
| K06 | Independent support | Do additional sources add a distinct evidential basis? | Origin comparison; several copies of one assertion are not independent confirmations. | S24; Stage 1 |
| K07 | Extraction fidelity | Are meaning, context and important qualifications retained? | Source-to-extraction checks and tested instructions; lost negation or altered denominator fails. | S09, S37 |
| K08 | Counterevidence treatment | Are material null, negative and conflicting findings visible? | Inclusion decisions and analysis of conflicts; silent exclusion to preserve a narrative fails. | S07, S32 |
| K09 | Inferential validity | Is the step from evidence to conclusion justified? | Explicit method and alternatives; a calculation or narrative that assumes its conclusion fails. | S12, S28 |
| K10 | Uncertainty communication | Are important limitations explicit without unsupported precision? | Claim-level explanation; one opaque confidence score cannot explain unlike uncertainties. | S13; Stage 1 |
| K11 | Temporal applicability | Is the finding valid for its stated period? | Observation/publication/access context and update rationale; a recent retrieval alone is insufficient. | S11, S22 |
| K12 | Review adequacy | Was the consequential work checked by an appropriate process? | Review target, findings and resolution; the presence of a checklist alone is insufficient. | S05, S30 |
| K13 | Reconstructability and reporting | Could another reader inspect how the answer was reached? | Methods, supporting links and limitations; polished prose without support fails. | S01, S15 |

## Candidate assessment posture

Assess dimensions separately and explain which apply to the research need. A severe unsupported material claim must not be offset by attractive formatting or plentiful citations. Conversely, a tightly bounded fact answer should not fail because it lacks a full systematic-review apparatus. These are design conclusions from the comparison, not a universal numerical rubric.

Some assessments can later use deterministic checks, such as whether a required locator is present. Others require substantive review, such as whether the cited passage actually supports a causal inference. A passing structural test can establish only its limited property. GRADE, CERQual, journalistic source policies and institutional review roles remain method-specific rather than being flattened into one universal grade.

Later evaluation design must decide how these candidates are exercised and evidenced. That later implementation responsibility does not leave this Stage 2 output incomplete: the current contract explicitly requests candidate quality dimensions, not a running benchmark suite.
