## S01: RULER publication identity

Hsieh, Sun, Kriman, Acharya, Rekesh, Jia, Zhang and Ginsburg, [arXiv:2404.06654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654). Inspected title, authors, abstract, comments and submission history. The page identifies v3 as 6 August 2024 and v2 as 11 April 2024; comments identify COLM 2024. It is a publication record, not an independent replication or a retraction database. No retraction notice was observed on this bounded record inspection; that is not a universal clearance.

## S02: RULER v2

Same authors, [version 2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06654v2), 11 April 2024. Inspected abstract and introduction. The abstract reports ten models and thirteen tasks. This is an earlier representation of the same work, not a second independent study. Unread appendices and visual results are not relied upon.

## S03: RULER v3

Same authors, [version 3 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06654v3), 6 August 2024. Inspected abstract, section 3 category description, section 4 setup/effective-context definition and Table 3 text/caption, and section 8 limitations passages. The main comparison uses seventeen models. The effective-context threshold is the Llama2-7B 4K baseline, reported as 85.6%. The source describes synthetic, bounded tasks rather than a complete real-use evaluation. No chart image, PDF, fresh inference run or present-day leaderboard is used. The design inference is that this threshold cannot by itself authorise a research application's acceptance rule.

## S04: Study and report linkage

Cochrane, [Handbook chapter 4](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04), sections 4.6.1–4.6.3; HTML inspected. Reports of a study should be linked while useful supplementary information is retained. The pack transfers identity/linkage discipline to scholarly evidence; it does not claim that a benchmark paper is a clinical trial or impose every Cochrane review procedure on a bounded paper check.

## S05: Horizon scanning

UK Government Office for Science, [The Futures Toolkit, 2024 edition](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/futures-toolkit-for-policy-makers-and-analysts/the-futures-toolkit-html), horizon-scanning prose, and [template](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/horizon-scanning-template/horizon-scanning-template), published 29 August 2024. The sources distinguish scoped scanning, evidence and time horizons. Their example scales are not calibrated probabilities. The proposed trend pack remains deferred until a longitudinal comparison demonstrates value beyond the core freshness/refresh rules. No continuous service is inferred.

## S07: Primary-source interpretation

Library of Congress, [Teacher's Guides and Analysis Tool](https://www.loc.gov/programs/teachers/getting-started-with-primary-sources/guides/). Search-indexed primary-page description inspected; linked PDF guides were not opened. Observation, reflection, questions and further investigation are distinct activities. Using that distinction for creative-reference research is a design adaptation, not validation of a creative-production methodology or rights clearance.

## S08: Investigative standards

Bellingcat, [Editorial Standards & Practices](https://www.bellingcat.com/about/editorial-standards-practices). HTML inspected, particularly verification, privacy/harm and generative-AI sections. Human checking and publication responsibilities matter; the organisation does not permit generated publication copy under the stated policy. This AI-authored design is not represented as satisfying Bellingcat's editorial process. The OSINT pack is deferred rather than claiming specialist investigative authority.

## S09: Technology landscape

WIPO, [Patent Analytics resources](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/patent-analytics), primary search catalogue text inspected. It identifies specialist landscape-method resources; it does not establish current database behaviour or validate patent-rights conclusions. The accepted Stage 2 M4/S20 comparison supplies the already-inspected historical method distinctions. No new reading of the linked guideline PDF is claimed here.

## S10: Repository-risk signals

OpenSSF, [Scorecard](https://scorecard.dev/), primary-page description and check categories inspected through search output. It distinguishes such checks as maintenance, dependency updates and CI. The design keeps these dimensions separate; it does not inherit an aggregate score as research truth or claim to have run Scorecard. Security assurance, task fit and licence applicability remain distinct.
