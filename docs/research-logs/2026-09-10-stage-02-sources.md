# Stage 2 Source Register

Access date: 10 September 2026. This register records the primary professional sources actually inspected, including bounded abstract or search-passage access. The companion research log describes discovery, exclusions and limitations. Source identifiers are stable within the Stage 2 document set; cited sections, not whole organisations, support each claim.

These are predominantly normative methods, professional standards and practitioner accounts. They establish what their authors prescribe or describe, not the prevalence or measured effectiveness of those practices. No external text is reproduced wholesale. Multiple chapters or organisational pages are separate locators, not automatically independent evidence.
