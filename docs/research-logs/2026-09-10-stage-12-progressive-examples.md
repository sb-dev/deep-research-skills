## 1. Five domain-native levels

### Level 1: Find and verify one bounded answer

Resolve one proposition with direct, inspectable support. Required emphasis: question framing, source selection, primary recovery, citation correctness, freshness and a finite verification scope. A document header, a package declaration and a data-release label require different interpretations even though each answer is small. A short answer still preserves uncertainty and exact source access.

### Level 2: Compare a small evidence set

Apply explicit criteria to a deliberately limited set of evidence. Required emphasis: multiple source classes, comparable units, independence, contradiction handling and small synthesis. The selected trio contrasts a service-policy decision, related scholarly reports and a numeric case comparison rather than repeating three tool rankings.

### Level 3: Produce a focused decision-ready report

Own a focused question from framing through evidence acquisition, analysis, a supported report and review. Required emphasis: method fit, question coverage, cross-source synthesis, limitations and decision relevance. The three examples span scholarly, market/problem and open-source technical evidence. Research informs the consuming owner; it does not commit that owner to action.

### Level 4: Handle contested, scaled and changing evidence

Diagnose ambiguity, duplication and temporal change across a substantial evidence set. Required emphasis: multi-hop retrieval, a large source set or structured corpus, conflict, longitudinal evidence, bounded follow-up and preservation during repair. Both natural defects and deliberately injected test mutations must retain their true provenance.

### Level 5: Run a research programme feeding production

Coordinate complementary questions and hand off evidence to other production disciplines. Required emphasis: the complete project thesis, programme-level consistency, source-preserving handoff, reusable learning and explicit consumer authority. The selected trio contrasts research before architecture, real-world science for fictional production, and an opportunity combining market, technical and editorial constraints.

Levels measure breadth of responsibility, not word count, spending, confidence or autonomy. Level 5 does not gain permission to publish, procure, implement, disclose private material or replace specialist judgement.

### Complementarity and residual concentration

At each level, the selected examples differ in their evidence operation, not only their topic. The repair thread intentionally progresses from release metadata to descriptive comparison, market-inference limits and longitudinal reconciliation. Its repeated source family is explicit; L5-04 was not selected to avoid extending that concentration further. RULER progresses from version linkage to multi-work synthesis, where repeating version metadata cannot answer the question.

Selection does not conceal execution constraints. A source, pack, dataset permission or competent reviewer may be unavailable in a later run. The run must record that fact rather than silently substituting an easier task or claiming the relevant behaviour passed.
