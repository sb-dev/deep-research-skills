## Synthesis method

The specifications were generated by ownership consolidation, not by copying one earlier log wholesale.

For each Stage 14 ownership list:

1. identify accepted decisions that belong to the responsibility;
2. preserve exact identifiers where they matter, including W01–W08, A01–A11, F01–F20, K01–K16 and the three selected skills;
3. assign one canonical owner for detailed semantics;
4. put only necessary cross-spec summary elsewhere;
5. retain evidence/maturity limitations from the accepted stages;
6. ensure implementation can proceed without reading conversation history.

Conflicts were resolved by accepted-stage precedence: later accepted design specialised earlier design only within its assigned responsibility. No accepted rule required a user decision.
