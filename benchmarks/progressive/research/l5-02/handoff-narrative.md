# Narrative owner evidence handoff — r2

Status: producer draft, pending separate fixed-input evaluation. Sources and historical periods: `research.md`; exact support and qualifications: `mechanism-evidence-map.md`.

Supported mechanisms: C1–C4 allow stories about delayed release, imperfect observation and different timescales; C5–C6 allow competing water goals without assuming a universal intervention outcome. Evidence constrains possibilities, not a plot ending.

Adaptable abstraction: a community may mistake an unusual water level for a known flow, debate upstream allocation, or compare a wet season with the following dry period. Attribution should remain contested until observations distinguish mechanisms. These are fictional proposals, not events reported by the sources.

Forest option (r2, C6/E6): a restoration dispute can now turn on differing water metrics and stakeholder goals. Treat the E6 tradeoff as a plausible premise to investigate, not a guaranteed resolution; characters need evidence of their own basin before attributing a joint outcome.

Unknowns and owner decisions: choose who has records, what they can measure, the timespan of the story and whether uncertainty is explicit in dialogue. If real historical institutions or water law are introduced, commission separate research; none is supplied here.

Continuity tests proposed: tag dialogue as character belief, supported narrator statement or hypothesis; check that a rainfall recurrence interval is not scripted as a guaranteed calendar event; preserve the difference between water quantity, timing and quality across scenes. A character can be wrong without the report endorsing the claim. No script or simulated outcome has been produced.

Before a plot-derived or game-emergent mechanism is represented as a real-world finding: restate it as a falsifiable hypothesis, specify alternative causes and collect relevant independent observations with expert review. A compelling story and a consistent simulation do not constitute an experiment.

Change triggers: altered climate/geology or use, a corrected source, a precision-dependent reading of S5 Fig.3, or any proposed real-world transfer reopens affected support. The owner controls production choices; this package confers no scientific or engineering approval.
