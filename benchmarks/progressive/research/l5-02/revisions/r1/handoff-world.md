# World and environment owner evidence handoff — r1

Status: producer draft, pending separate fixed-input evaluation. Sources and historical periods: `research.md`; exact support and qualifications: `mechanism-evidence-map.md`.

Supported mechanisms: C1 supplies storage/transfer vocabulary; C2 supplies an example of contrasting snow and rain seasonality; C6 supplies human pathways. These support environmental coherence, without transferring Swiss coefficients to a fictional landscape.

Adaptable abstraction: sketch an upland snow store, porous soils, an explicitly assumed groundwater system and a lowland river with settlements, diversions and return flows. Place a dam or withdrawal as a choice that changes the water ledger. Landscape proportions and river sizes remain fictional; the global USGS drawing is not a catchment cross-section to copy literally.

Forest option: leave the direction of infiltration and event-peak changes open until G1 obtains the Fig.5 metric context. Keep annual yield, visible wetness and water quality separate in the environment brief.

Unknowns and owner decisions: select geology/permeability, climatic seasonality, whether the groundwater divide follows the topographic divide, ecological timescales and the degree of realism. Groundwater direction, wetlands, sediment loads and water chemistry cannot be inferred solely from an attractive terrain map. These items need research if they become material scientific claims.

Production checks proposed: trace where every depicted source and sink connects, check season consistency, distinguish river stage from flow, and review whether a visual cue accidentally asserts a universal land-cover effect. No terrain or hydrological simulation was built.

Before transferring a generated environment result to reality: obtain topography, geology, soils, observed weather/flows and appropriate ecological/chemical data for the actual setting; examine resolution, seasonal coverage and uncertainty with qualified specialists. A plausible illustration or simulated habitat is not empirical validation.

Change triggers: altered climate/geology or use, a corrected source, a precision-dependent reading of S5 Fig.3, or any proposed real-world transfer reopens affected support. The owner controls production choices; this package confers no scientific or engineering approval.
