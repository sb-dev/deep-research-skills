# Game owner evidence handoff — r1

Status: producer draft, pending separate fixed-input evaluation. Sources and historical periods: `research.md`; exact support and qualifications: `mechanism-evidence-map.md`.

Supported mechanisms: use C1–C4 to distinguish stores, rates, event duration and observation proxies. Read the source-specific boundaries in `mechanism-evidence-map.md` before translating them into rules.

Adaptable abstraction: choose a basin with snow, soil, groundwater and channel stores and declare one clock. Account for all internal and boundary transfers; cap a transfer by available water. Preserve positive stores and carry unused water forward. The game owner chooses whether hidden stores are visible to players. These are design proposals, not implementation instructions or a calibrated model.

Forest option: withhold an infiltration/peak-response bonus until G1 supplies its metric context. Annual water yield must remain a separate production variable from any future benefit. Do not use S5 case percentages as numerical effect sizes.

Unknowns and owner decisions: choose time compression, spatial resolution, weather generator and how much parameter uncertainty to expose. Event timing and isotope age are distinct; a fast flood animation must not imply that every water parcel is fresh rainfall. No storm sequence or hydrograph has been generated here.

Tests before adoption: dimensional/unit checks; zero-input conservation; internal transfer cancellation; withdrawals with explicit returns; sensitivity to timestep and initial storage; independent wet/dry and snow/no-snow scenarios. These tests assess software bookkeeping, not empirical accuracy. No tests have been run because no simulation exists.

Before any real-world claim: identify a real basin, observe forcing and discharge/storage proxies over relevant seasons/events, establish calibration versus held-out validation periods, quantify measurement and parameter uncertainty, compare alternatives and obtain hydrological appraisal. A surprising emergent interaction becomes a proposed hypothesis and observation plan, not a discovered natural law.

Change triggers: altered climate/geology or use, a corrected source, a precision-dependent reading of S5 Fig.3, or any proposed real-world transfer reopens affected support. The owner controls production choices; this package confers no scientific or engineering approval.
