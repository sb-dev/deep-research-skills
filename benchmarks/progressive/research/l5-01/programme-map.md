# Archival-research programme map

Revision r2, 2026-09-13. Proposed programme for owner review; based on [research.md](research.md) r2, C1–C11. No architecture is designed or approved. The bounded UK/US survey supports the questions below; it does not establish worldwide demand or professional sufficiency.

| Workstream and question | Evidence and proposed investigation | Reviewable output / completion condition | Receiving owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provenance and context — Q1 | C1/C6/C8. Examine one permitted record's creator, function, custody, arrangement and description history. Distinguish archival provenance from the provenance of our own extraction. | Context note with established, suspected and unknown relationships; expert verifies interpretation-relevant links. | Archival research lead; provenance specialist for formal mapping |
| Discovery — Q2 | C2/C5/C9 and X1. Compare keyword, creator/function and hierarchical routes on one bounded collection. Keep query, filters, date, result boundary and actual access. | Search account showing why routes differ and where coverage stops; resolve syntax before reliance. | Research lead; software owner validates mechanics |
| Missing records — Q3 | C2/C3/C5/C9. Separate no match, undescribed, not digitised, unprocessed, restricted, misfiled, retained elsewhere and evidenced destruction. Treat unknown causes as unknown. | Missingness account with source for every asserted cause; absent catalogue result never becomes event absence. | Archivist decides historical meaning |
| Representations — Q4 | C4/C6/C8. Pilot description → permitted image/original → transcription → research extract, including page/region and transformations. | Evidence chain that exposes access depth and disputed readings; image/text comparison by a competent reader. | Research lead; transcription/language specialist |
| Interpretation — Q5 | C10. Test two plausible readings against original context, contrary records and known source dependence; seek voices outside the surviving administrative account where authorised. | Claim/alternative note with inferential steps and unresolved selection bias. | Historian/archivist; community/domain reviewer where appropriate |
| Privacy and rights — Q6 | C7/C8, G4. Define intended use, item-specific restrictions, representations, audience and processors before sensitive work. | Permitted-use decision attached to exact item/use; any unresolved decision remains visible. | Rights/privacy owner; qualified legal advice where needed |
| Review and revisions — Q7 | C10, G1–G6. Separate producer checks, fixed-input evidence audit and expert interpretation. Test a changed catalogue description or corrected transcription. | Review identifies exact revision and changed dependencies; unaffected evidence survives. | Independent evaluator; archival lead accepts use |
| Reusable specialisation — Q8 | C11 and S0. Use the qualification and showcase below as a development proposal. | [pack-qualification.md](pack-qualification.md) comparison requirements satisfied before any promotion. | Pack maintainer; separate evaluator |

Suggested order: the programme owner first chooses a bounded archival question and intended use. Research and the archival lead then assemble one lawful evidence chain and a missingness example. Rights decisions precede restricted acquisition or reuse. Software and UX owners use the reviewed evidence to decide what to prototype, and return infeasible or ambiguous requirements to research. Pack comparison can use the same lawful fixture after the archival method is reviewed; it cannot replace that review.

The minimum pilot should answer whether context navigation and representation tracking improve the specific task, where they cost time, and whether users can distinguish absence from unavailable evidence. This is a proposed learning goal, not measured effectiveness. Scope expansion to community archives, other countries/languages, bulk OCR or preservation services needs a revised source strategy and owner decisions.

No domain repository, common data model, product roadmap, user research recruitment, API connector or production pack has been created. Receiving owners retain implementation, interaction design, professional interpretation and approval. Explicit handoffs are [software](handoff-software.md), [UX](handoff-ux.md) and [review needs](review-needs.md).
