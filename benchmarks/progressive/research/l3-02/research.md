### Operational hypotheses and alternatives

A volunteer recorder might benefit from fewer fields and reliable post-event export; a group organiser might need event/volunteer coordination; a network analyst might need comparable impact records. These are candidate users inferred from C3/C5/C6/C7. The treasurer, trustees, host organisation or network sponsor might control purchases, but none is verified as a buyer. A service user bringing a broken appliance is not thereby a software customer.

Prefer testing an add-on or improved documentation before a standalone system: Fixometer/Restarters.net and RepairMonitor already cover substantial parts of the proposed workflow. Paper notes plus an existing spreadsheet/export process remain a testable baseline, not an observed universal practice. Parts availability, opening products and missing repair information are alternative explanations for repair failure in the [ORA 2024 executive summary](https://openrepair.org/open-data/insights/2024-report/); an administration tool would not directly fix these physical constraints. Its barrier percentages are not reused because their denominators need further inspection.

Technically, retain source/provider IDs, event dates, country, original and mapped categories, missing values and outcome distinctions. Preserve optionality; never turn unknown into failure. Keep attendee/contact records separate from public repair records. The repository's stated data licence is relevant to reuse, not automatic permission for all website material, personal data or supplier integrations. A scoped export check still needs source-specific licence/terms review and an actual sample; neither implementation feasibility nor legal clearance is certified here.

### Decision, smallest experiments and reversal

Consumer decision owners retain authority over any participant research, spending or business start. Proposed, not executed: (1) with later consent, observe one complete workflow at each of three varied UK groups, including an existing-platform satisfied group; time recording and corrections and record current workaround; (2) show the same tiny export/form change and existing-tool option, then ask organisers to choose and explain, avoiding leading “would you pay?” questions; (3) separately establish who can approve expenditure, what competing spend it displaces and whether any budgeted commitment exists. Compare with an existing-tool documentation improvement.

Advance only if several independent groups exhibit the same consequential recurring burden, existing options cannot reasonably address it, users voluntarily repeat the trial, and an authorised payer offers a credible budget path. Reverse to no-product/support-only if burden is minor, incumbent configuration solves it, data entry harms repair time, buyers cannot be identified, or maintenance/rights costs exceed credible value. These are proposed decision rules and value judgements, not statistical validation thresholds. No interviews, forecast, market sizing, purchases or contact occurred.
